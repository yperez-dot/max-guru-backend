# Devoted HRA incentive — effective March 1, 2026

Source: Agent Medicare Hub — https://agentmedicarehub.com/carrier-ops/devoted-hra

This Carrier Ops page is the source of truth for Devoted HRA pay questions. Cite the Hub page instead of a plan-grid cell.

- For applications written **March 1, 2026 or later**, the **$50** incentive applies only to HRAs on **SNP plans: C-SNP or D-SNP**.
- The HRA must be completed within **5 calendar days of enrollment submission**.
- Devoted no longer pays $50 for non-SNP HRAs.
- Continue encouraging HRAs for all Devoted members; non-SNP HRAs are unpaid.
- Source basis: Devoted broker HRA FAQ / partner notices; CareValue, Fidelis, and HealthSherpa notices align.
