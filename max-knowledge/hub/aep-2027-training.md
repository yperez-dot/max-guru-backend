## What is AEP?

The one window most clients use to change Medicare coverage.

Key dates

### October 15 – December 7, 2026

Coverage starts January 1, 2027

### What clients can do

- Switch from one Medicare Advantage plan to another

- Leave Original Medicare and join a Medicare Advantage plan

- Drop Medicare Advantage and go back to Original Medicare (with or without Part D)

- Join, switch, or drop a Part D drug plan

Remember

They can submit more than one application — CMS keeps the last one . Last application wins.

## What's new this year

Six changes that actually affect how you sell. That's it.

SOA 

### No more 48-hour wait

Get the SOA, then you can meet the same day. Starts October 1, 2026.

SOA 

### E-sign is fine

Wet signature is not required. Phone or video records count for remote appointments.

### Collect SOAs at educational events

You can take an SOA at an educational event. You still cannot sell during the educational part.

### No 12-hour event gap

You can go from educational to marketing in the same place the same day. Tell people first. Let them leave.

Part D 

### $2,400 drug cap

In 2027, Part D out-of-pocket costs cap at $2,400. After that, the client pays $0 for covered drugs.

Calls 

### Disclaimer before benefits

Say the TPMO disclaimer before you talk plan benefits — not on a 60-second clock.

Module 5 · New for 2027

## SOA in plain English

The questions agents actually ask.

Do I still need an SOA? Yes. Dropping the 48-hour wait did not drop the SOA. Get it before every personal marketing appointment. 

Do I still wait 48 hours? No. Starting October 1, 2026, you can meet the same day you get the SOA — for 2027 enrollments. 

Does it have to be a wet signature? No. Electronic signatures meet the “in writing” rule for in-person appointments. 

Can I reuse an SOA? Yes, for 12 months, as long as you only discuss the products listed on it. You can use it for more than one appointment. 

When do I need a new one? When you want to talk about a product that is not on the original SOA. Also if a different agent is running the appointment — that agent needs their own SOA. 

New plan year — new SOA? Not automatically. If the SOA does not name a plan year, it is still good into 2027 if it is within 12 months and the product list is the same. If it says “2026 plans only,” get a new one for 2027. 

Easy habit

Use a generic SOA (MA + Part D), not a carrier-specific one. Keep a copy. SOA records are kept 10 years.

Module 6 · New for 2027

## Events got simpler

Educational and marketing can now happen back-to-back.

Old rule 
You had to wait 12 hours between an educational event and a marketing event at the same location.

2027 rule 
That 12-hour gap is gone. You can switch to a marketing event right after — same room, same day.

### Do this when you switch

- Tell the room the educational part is over and a sales event is starting

- Give people a clear chance to leave before you market

- You may collect SOAs during the educational event

- Do not sell a specific plan during the educational portion

### Still true

- File the event with the carrier so it gets into HPMS — usually 10 days ahead

- Label it correctly: educational or marketing

- No meals as a reason to enroll. Light snacks are not a loophole for a sales pitch.

Module 7 · New for 2027

## What to tell clients

Keep this simple at the kitchen table.

Part D 2027

### Drug costs cap at $2,400

Once they hit $2,400 out of pocket on covered Part D drugs, they pay $0 for the rest of the year.

- No coverage gap. Part D is deductible → initial coverage → catastrophic. After the cap, they are done paying. 

- They can spread payments. The Medicare Prescription Payment Plan lets them pay drug costs monthly instead of all at the pharmacy. 

- Always open the ANOC. Premiums, networks, and extras can still change even with the cap. Compare 2026 vs 2027 before you enroll. 

- Do not promise a plan will stay the same. MA payments and plan designs shifted for 2027. Confirm benefits in the approved material. 

Agent tip

Run a NEEDS check every time: Needs, Eligibility, Affordability, Decision. Then explain that signing the app means they are enrolling — not “just applying.”

Module 8

## Marketing rules that still matter

Some 2027 rules loosened. These did not.

- No cold calls, door knocks, or unsolicited texts/DMs

- No marketing in exam rooms or at the pharmacy counter

- No meals or gifts to get someone to enroll

- No “free,” “you’ll lose coverage,” or other scare language

- No selling life insurance or annuities in the AEP Medicare appointment

### 2027 tweaks

- TPMO disclaimer: say it before you discuss plan benefits — not on a 60-second timer

- SHIP referral no longer required in the TPMO disclaimer — you can drop that sentence, but Medicare.gov / 1-800-MEDICARE must stay

- Words like “best” or “top” are not automatically banned — but they still cannot be misleading. Stick to approved copy.

- Keep sales-call recordings 6 years (was 10). Keep SOAs 10 years.

- 1:1 consent is still required before you share a beneficiary’s data with another TPMO

Materials

Only carrier-approved and CMS-approved pieces. Do not edit them. YourFMO and the carrier portals are the source.

Pending from CMS

CMS confirmed the SHIP line is going away but hasn't published the updated Medicare Communications and Marketing Guidelines (MCMG) with new verbatim disclaimer text yet. Until that drops, keep using your current SHIP-inclusive disclaimer — it's still compliant, just no longer required to include SHIP.

Module 9

## What you CAN do

Permitted work during AEP 2027.

- Meet the same day after the SOA is on file

- Collect an SOA at an educational event

- Move from educational to marketing the same day, after you tell the room and let people leave

- Talk only about products listed on that SOA

- Use approved materials from carrier portals / YourFMO

- Follow up with people who already gave permission to contact

- Enroll in Sunfire or the carrier portal

Before you market

AHIP + every required carrier cert must show Ready-to-Sell. No cert, no AEP.

Module 10

## What you CANNOT do

These still get agents in trouble.

- Market a product that is not on the SOA

- Promise benefits the carrier materials do not show

- Change or homemade marketing pieces

- Call, text, or DM someone who did not give permission

- Sell in a healthcare setting that is off-limits

- Use a meal, gift, or “limited time” scare to push an enrollment

If you break the rules

Contract termination, CMS fines, and sanctions. Your license is the business — protect it.

## Clean enrollment, every time

Same process. Faster SOA step.

- 1
Get the SOA Before any marketing conversation. E-sign is fine. Same-day meeting is fine. 

- 2
Hold the appointment Only the products on the SOA. Use approved materials. 

- 3
Enroll in Sunfire or the carrier site Check names, MBI, and plan ID. Last app submitted is the one CMS keeps. 

- 4
Confirm it landed Make sure the client gets the carrier confirmation. 

- 5
Keep the file SOA, notes, and what you showed them. SOA: 10 years. Call recordings: 6 years. 

Effective date

Apps in by December 7, 2026 start January 1, 2027.
