# Client Forms & PDFs

Official SSA/CMS forms you can share with clients for enrollment, employer verification, and IRMAA appeals.

## SSA-44 — IRMAA Appeal (Life-Changing Event)

Most Used 
SSA 

Use when a client has a qualifying life-changing event (e.g., retirement, reduced work hours, loss of pension)
and wants Social Security to reconsider IRMAA.

Open PDF (https://www.ssa.gov/forms/ssa-44.pdf)

## CMS-40B — Application for Medicare Part B

CMS 
Enrollment 

Use when a client needs to enroll in Part B (medical insurance).
Often paired with the employer verification form when using a Special Enrollment Period.

Open PDF (https://www.cms.gov/medicare/cms-forms/cms-forms/downloads/cms40b-e.pdf)

## CMS-L564 — Employer Verification (Group Health Plan)

CMS 
Most Requested 

Employer completes this to verify group coverage. Commonly used with CMS-40B
to request Part B under a Special Enrollment Period.

Open PDF (https://www.cms.gov/Medicare/CMS-Forms/CMS-Forms/Downloads/CMSL564-E.PDF)

## Part A & Part B — Special Enrollment (Exceptional Conditions)

CMS 
Special Circumstances 

Use when a client needs enrollment due to special circumstances/exceptional conditions.
Helpful when standard SEP rules don't apply cleanly.

Open PDF (https://www.cms.gov/files/document/application-medicare-part-part-b-special-enrollment-period-exceptional-conditions.pdf)

### Quick Guidance

- Enrolling into Part B? Start with CMS-40B . If client had employer coverage, also use CMS-L564 .

- Client paying higher Part B premium (IRMAA)? Use SSA-44 if a life-changing event applies.

- Complex situation / unusual timing? Use the Special Enrollment (Exceptional Conditions) form.

Note: These are official SSA/CMS documents. Always confirm the form is signed where required before submission.
