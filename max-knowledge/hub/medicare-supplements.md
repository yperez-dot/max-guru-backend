About these documents

Most carrier documents on this page are UHC/AARP-specific (rates, application, hospital network). However, plan letter rules (G, N, HD-G, etc.), Guaranteed Issue rights, and the Part B deductible are standardized by CMS — they apply the same way across all carriers. Use the UHC docs for UHC enrollments; the quick reference rules apply regardless of carrier.

### Quick Reference — General Medigap

Open Enrollment Period 
6 months from Part B effective date. Guaranteed issue — no health questions. Best time to sell.

GI Events — 63-Day Rule 
Loss of creditable coverage, MA trial right. App received within 63 days of termination. Include docs.

Part B Deductible 2026 
$257/year. Plan G does NOT cover it. Plan F does — but only for pre-2020 Medicare eligibility.

Plans C & F — Restricted 
Only for clients eligible for Medicare before Jan 1, 2020. Everyone else → push to Plan G.

### UHC / AARP Quick Reference

Plan G Select vs Plan G 
Same benefits, lower premium. Requires network hospital use for non-emergency care. Good fit for clients near participating hospitals.

Underwriting Knockouts (§6) 
YES = ineligible. Cancer, cardiac, COPD, ALS, Alzheimer's, transplants. Review with client before submitting.

AARP Membership — UHC Only 
Required for UHC plans only. Other carriers (Aetna, Cigna, Mutual of Omaha, etc.) don't require it. For UHC: $20/year at aarp.org/savetoday or 1-866-331-1964. Agents cannot pay dues for clients. Client can cancel membership after policy is issued — coverage continues as long as premiums are paid.

Discounts 
3% multi-insured (two on same account) + $2/mo EFT OR $24/yr annual pay. EFT and annual pay cannot be combined.

Rate Areas — Florida 
Area 1 = South FL (Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach). Area 2 = rest of FL. ~$55–60/mo difference on Plan G.

Submitting Applications 
JarvisEnroll preferred at uhcjarvis.com. Paper mail to PO Box 105331, Atlanta GA 30348. Fax: 1-888-836-3985. No email submissions accepted.

Producer Help Desk 
1-888-381-8581 (Option 2 = Español). Also available via Chat on Jarvis.

Member Services (clients) 
1-800-523-5800. Verify hospital network status before any Plan G Select enrollment — networks can change.
