# AgentSync

Use AgentSync to request contracts/appointments, upload required documents, and track your contracting status.

## Quick Actions

Most agents only need the 3 actions below.

Watch Walkthrough
(https://docs.google.com/videos/d/1fjgTAloT029p6Etls4AwZ0t10gTS60yxhG6rukPNvGs/edit)

Download Onboarding Guide
(https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1d10ABfx4q0UZE5eRPWzBXKR7e0O7QmrTK7jcjTxIs4w/edit?usp=sharing)

Before you submit: Make sure your AHIP is complete (if required), your license is active, and your E&O is current.

## Before You Submit (Checklist)

- Have your license number and NPN ready.

- Upload active E&O (not expired).

- Upload any required documents (IDs, certificates, etc.).

- If you're a transfer agent , have your release letter ready (carrier-specific).

- After submitting, check your email (and spam) for carrier approval updates.

## What Happens Next?

- Most carriers review within 3–10 business days .

- Transfers can take longer depending on release requirements.

- You'll receive approval emails directly from carriers.

## Common Issues

- Expired E&O uploaded

- AHIP not completed first (when required)

- Transfer submitted without release letter

- Carrier emails going to spam

Tip: If you're waiting on an update, allow up to 10 business days before requesting a status check.

## Still Need Help?

Please review the checklist.

- AHIP completed (if required)

- Active E&O uploaded

- Release letter uploaded (if transfer)

- Allowed 3–10 business days

- Checked spam folder
