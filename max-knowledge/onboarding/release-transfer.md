# THEI agency–agent release and transfer by carrier

Nextere carriers (Doctors, HealthSun, Solis) sit under New Health Partners. YourPlanChoice, LLC carriers use the FAQ rows below.

---

## Aetna

- **Release type:** Full release required. Top of hierarchy must release. No partial releases. Corp release covers downlines if the Downline box is checked on the Aetna MA/PDP release form **and** those downlines are not the same contract level as the moving agent (otherwise they roll to the next upline). Written release, or 90-day intent to transfer.
- **Contract level / upline change:** new link at the target level. Agent must move at the same level; can upgrade after 90 days.
- **Blackout:** 10/1 – 12/31. No transfers during AEP; requests made during AEP wait until January 1.
- **Notes:** All pending business resolved first. Commissions / book follow the agent.

## Anthem (Elevance / New Health Partners release path)

Anthem (NHP):

- Immediate release if approved electronically. Automatic release if the upline does not approve within **10 days**. One release every 12 months without upline approval.
- Upload the release during contracting. If none uploaded, Anthem notifies the current upline for electronic approval.
- **Blackout:** July 1 – September 30. Requests in that window are held until **January 2**. New appointments during blackout still need an approved release.

(YourPlanChoice **Elevance** row is a separate FMO path — see Elevance below.)

## Clover

- Agents may change uplines **once per calendar year**, effective the **1st of the month** (signed release Jan 3 → Feb 1; Notice of Intent Jan 3 → May 1).
- New Intake Form + new contracting link after transfer. Re-upgrade timing is Clover’s discretion.
- **Blackout:** 10/1 – 12/31.

## Devoted

- **Release type:** Full release required. See Devoted Broker Manual p.5: https://assets.devoted.com/brokers/Devoted_Health-Broker_Manual.pdf
- No fixed blackout for **immediate** release. Delayed releases from Aug 15 are not effective until Dec 8.
- **THEI / NHP portal steps (Devoted transfer process):** In the Devoted broker portal: left menu **Messages** → **ADD NEW MESSAGE** → category **RELEASE & TRANSFER REQUEST**. Attach the release. Message template: attaching a release from (current upline); change upline to **Your Plan Choice** (agency NPN: 1904190). **Must attach the release.**
- Also: written request to current upline; 7–10 days; no transfers Oct 1 – Dec 31; first-of-month effective; renewals stay with agent; no outstanding chargebacks; may recertify.

## Doctors (Doctors HealthCare / Doctors HealthPlan)

- Standard / full release from current FMO or upline. Good standing, no compliance violations.
- Written request to current upline with full name, NPN, and reason.
- **Timing:** 5–10 business days. **No transfers during AEP (Oct 15 – Dec 7).**
- Pending applications must be done. Book transfers with the agent. No debt to current upline.

## Elevance (YourPlanChoice / FAQ)

- Written release, or approved electronic release once new appointment paperwork is submitted. If the prior upline does not grant release, a **90-day** period starts the day paperwork was submitted. One release every 12 months. Email release requests no longer accepted.
- **Blackout:** 7/1 – 9/30; release effective 1/1.
- Level change = new commission schedule/transmittal. External transfer level is Elevance’s discretion; internal affiliate-to-affiliate stays at the same level for 90 days before upgrading.

## Florida Blue

- Level change = new commission schedule; same-company hierarchy change = new transmittal; affiliate-to-affiliate follows transfer guidelines. Internal TPA forms path is under `2024_Medicare_Advantage/FL_Blue/Transfers`.
- **Blackout:** Sept 1 – Dec 31.
- 10–15 business days; book stays with agent; no outstanding debt.

## Freedom

- Request release from current upline. If unobtainable, forward the emailed request to AgentLicense@freedomh.com. If no business in the last 90 days, released to the new upline after **4 days** with proof of the request to the old upline.
- Must complete an AX contract; marketer sends VIP codes for https://vipagentsupport.com/Account/Login.
- No blackout listed.

## Gold Kidney

- Immediate release **or** delayed release (90 days).
- Complete Upline Change Request Form. Release letter required if upline is **not** Gold Kidney. Email brokers@goldkidney.com and contracting@healthexps.com.
- Request on/before the 15th → transfer next month; after the 15th → the following month. Delayed = 90 days then transfer.
- **Blackout:** Oct 15 – Jan 1. No releases/transfers effective during blackout. Agent may sell during the 90-day wait. Overrides stay with current upline until transfer.

## HCSC

- New Agent X TPA contract required. Internal forms path under `HCSC_BCBS/Transfer-Release`.
- Affiliate/FMO change window **6/1 – 7/31** annually (subject to HCSC change). Can upgrade immediately after transfer.

## HealthSpring (Cigna)

- Top-of-hierarchy release on current topline agency letterhead, valid **60 days**. **90-day self-release:** Request for Release form to CARL; clock starts when CARL receives it. Agent may keep selling during the 90 days; business stays under the hierarchy in place at sale. Moves must process within 60 days of eligibility or a new request/wait is required.
- **Blackout:** 9/15 – 12/31.
- Agency transfers to same or lower contract level; upgrade after 6 months if engagement criteria are met.

## HealthSun

- Immediate release (with letter), 3-month delayed if denied, or immediate if no active book.
- Release letter: FMO letterhead, signed, dated within **60 days**. Complete HealthSun Broker Contract. Send to contracting@carepointinsurance.com and contracting@healthexps.com. **New contract required in all cases.**
- Active book + no release: 3-month wait. No active book: immediate. **No transfers Jul 1 – Sep 30.** Requests after Oct 1 effective Jan 1.

## Humana MGA

- MGA-level release. Written release or 3-month intent to transfer — both need the **MGA Assignment / ABTF** form to agentsupport@humana.com. No new contract unless termed.
- **Blackout:** 7/11 – 1/1 (varies immediate vs not — see Humana blackout chart).
- The “USE MGA form!” process is **Humana MGA**.

## Humana SA

- Written release or 3-month intent to transfer; current contract is terminated. New TPA contract via Agent X.
- **Blackout:** 10/1 – 12/31.
- External transfer level is Humana’s discretion; internal stays at same level 90 days before upgrading.

## Kaiser

- Agent must term with current FMO and re-submit via the new FMO. Renewals stay with the prior FMO / principal; credit of sale transfers only with a change to the base medical plan.
- No blackout listed.

## Molina

- 90 days with constructive release, or 1st of the following month with immediate written release. No new contract — info is entered in Molina’s system.
- **Blackout:** 10/1 – 12/31.

## Optimum

- Same pattern as Freedom: request from current upline; if unobtainable, forward to AgentLicense@freedomh.com; no business in 90 days → released after 4 days with proof. AX contract + VIP codes at vipagentsupport.com.
- No blackout listed.

## Solis

- Immediate release if no prior business. Release letter if prior business exists. Automatic release after **3 months** if the upline denies (from proof of request).
- Email salessupport@solishealthplans.com, cc contracting@healthexps.com. State the request to align under **New Health Plans, LLC**.
- **No blackout period.**

## UnitedHealthcare (UHC, United, MedicareMax, Preferred Care Partner)

- **Release type:** **ITM (Intent to Move)** or written release. 90-day intent to transfer, or written release. New contracting at the **same level** (upgrade after 12 months). Hierarchy level changes submitted electronically through **Producer Express**.
- **Blackout:** 9/1 – 12/31.

How THEI submits:

- Written release **or** ITM if a release cannot be obtained.
- Release: on upline or NMA letterhead, signed by authorized signer, identifies the producer, dated within last **90 days**. Send to contracting@healthexps.com and christyne@levelupinsurancegroup.com.
- **ITM:** email shcerts@uhc.com, cc contracting@healthexps.com. Include producer name, writing number or NPN, Party ID, new upline name and writing number if available.
- ITM: mandatory **3-month** wait; may keep writing; must have been contracted with current upline **at least 6 months** (if less than 6 months, release is required). ITM allowed for IMO agents/agencies; **not** allowed for ICA agents/agencies.

## Wellcare

- Agent requests transfer in their portal. If upline does not grant: **14-day** delay for brokers changing uplines, **45-day** delay for agencies. New Agent X TPA contract required.
- **Blackout:** broker blackout 9/1 – 12/31. 

## Zing

- See Agent Transfer/Release Policy in THEI Drive. Same-day/immediate transfer noted; new intake form + new contracting link required.
- No blackout listed.

## Unattributed release processes (do not guess the carrier)

These release processes have **no carrier name**. **Do not attribute these to a carrier.**

The “USE MGA form!” process is **Humana MGA** (above), not unattributed.

### Unattributed process A 

- Full release required; good standing mandatory; no pending investigations.
- Formal written request to current FMO with all agent identifiers.
- 5–10 business days; AEP blackout applies; first-of-month effective date.
- Renewals transfer with agent; must clear all debts; new upline training required.

### Unattributed process B (generic)

- Full release required; good standing mandatory; active certification needed.
- Written request to upline with credentials and new upline specified.
- 5–10 business days; AEP blackout applies; first of month effective.
- Renewals transfer with agent; must clear all debts; training may be required.

### Unattributed process C 

- Full release from current FMO; active status; clean compliance record.
- Email or written request to current upline with NPN and agent ID.
- 10–15 business days; AEP blackout Oct 15 – Dec 7; first-of-month effective.
- Agent must request the upline change through the broker portal.

Questions on any release: contracting@healthexps.com.
