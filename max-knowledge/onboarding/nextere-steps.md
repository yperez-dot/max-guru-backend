# Nextere onboarding steps (Doctors, HealthSun, Solis)

**Nextere is only for Doctors HealthPlan, HealthSun, and Solis.** Do not use Nextere for UnitedHealthcare / UHC, Humana, Aetna, or other YourPlanChoice carriers — those go through AgentSync / AgentX / the carrier portal.

**Tenant: always enter `NHP`.** That field must be consistent across accounts.

Agency shown on the contract request: **The Health Experts Insurance**. Line of business: **Medicare**.

---

## STEP 1 — Accept the email invitation

Invitation comes from the agency contact. Click **Set Up Account**. The link expires — register promptly. If the link fails, use the reset URL in the email or contact support.

## STEPS 2–5 — Create the account

1. **Tenant:** enter **NHP** (required).
2. Registration: first name, last name, username, email, password, confirm password.
3. Accept Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
4. Complete reCAPTCHA and **Sign Up**.

## STEPS 6–8 — Verify email and sign in

Nextere sends a confirmation email. Refresh if the app prompts for an update. Sign in with email/username + password and reCAPTCHA.

## STEPS 9–10 — Verify NIPR credentials

In **Verify NIPR**, the form asks for the agent’s SSN and NPN so Nextere can pull licenses from the National Insurance Producer Registry. After **Verify with NIPR**, review the state map (license numbers, issue/expiration, residence) and **Continue**.

Do not store those identifiers in Max or in this repo.

## STEPS 11–13 — Update agent profile

Complete personal information, business address, and residential address (and whether they match), plus phone with country code. **Update**, then **Continue**.

## STEP 14 — Complete onboarding

When **Onboarding Complete!** appears, click **Ok**. Log out and log back in so profile settings apply before contracts.

## STEPS 15–18 — Contracts section

After logging back in: left sidebar **Contracts** → open the carrier request form → **LOB = Medicare**.

## STEPS 19–23 — Set up the Medicare contract

1. **Carrier:** only **Doctors HealthPlan**, **HealthSun**, or **Solis**.
2. **States:** Florida, Kansas, Missouri, and/or Texas, as applicable.
3. Say whether currently contracted with that carrier.
4. **View Licenses** for the selected state(s) before continuing.

## STEPS 24–26 — Finalize the request

Select **No** on “currently contracted / ready to sell” **only if** they are not already contracted and RTS with that carrier. **No** starts a new contract request (not “add state to an existing contract”) and a wrong answer delays the application.

Confirm then **Save**:

- Agency: The Health Experts Insurance
- LOB: Medicare
- Carrier: Doctors / HealthSun / Solis
- State (example): Florida
- Contract status: No (when it is a new contract)

Updates come by email. Transfers often need a release letter uploaded when prompted.
